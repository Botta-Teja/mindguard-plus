
MINDGUARD+ MASTER PROJECT DOCUMENT
==================================

PROJECT NAME
------------
MindGuard+ — AI Mental Wellness Companion

PROJECT PURPOSE
---------------
MindGuard+ is a full-stack AI-powered mental wellness platform designed as:
- Final Year Project
- Internship Showcase Project
- Resume Project
- Startup-style Product Prototype

The platform helps users:
- Chat with an AI mental wellness assistant
- Track emotions and moods
- Detect high-risk emotional states
- Alert emergency contacts
- Track mood history
- Set goals and reminders
- Receive wellness guidance

INTERVIEW EXPLANATION
==================================================

Project:
MindGuard+

Description:
Developed a full-stack AI-powered mental wellness platform using Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, JWT Authentication, bcrypt, Ollama AI (Phi3), Nodemailer, and Chart.js. The platform includes AI mental wellness chat, emotion detection, suicide-risk alerts, emergency email notifications, mood analytics, goal tracking, and automated reminder systems.
